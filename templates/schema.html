Star & Snowflake Schema

Fact_Food_Surplus is the central grain connecting multidimensional food rescue records.

⭐ Star Schema (Fact_Food_Surplus Centric) Yellow = [PK] | Blue = [FK] | Green = [M] Measures

⭐ Star Schema Architecture

Centralized Fact_Food_Surplus surrounded by completely denormalized dimension tables.

  • Direct Joins: Every dimension is exactly 1 join away from the fact table.
  • Query Speed: Optimized for rapid OLAP roll-up queries and slice/dice operations.
  • Tradeoff: High data redundancy in categorical columns (e.g., repeating Food_Category).

❄️ Snowflake Schema Architecture

Normalizes dimensions into Third Normal Form (3NF) by decomposing hierarchies into child tables.

  • Normalized Levels: Dim_Food joins to Dim_Food_Category; Dim_Date joins to Dim_Month.
  • Storage Optimization: Eliminates repeating text strings and update anomalies.
  • Tradeoff: Analytical queries require complex multi-table joins.
